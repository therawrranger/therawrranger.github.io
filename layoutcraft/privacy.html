LayoutCraft Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

LayoutCraft is a viewer for GDSII and OASIS chip layout files, made by rawrranger (“I”, “me”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use LayoutCraft on Android or on a computer.

Summary

LayoutCraft does not collect, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not use the internet. Your layout files never leave your device unless you share them yourself.

Files you open

  • LayoutCraft opens only the files you choose: in its own file browser, which shows its own folder and the folders you have allowed it to read through Android’s folder picker; through another app that picks files; or when you open a file with LayoutCraft from another app.
  • Layouts are read and displayed on your device. They are never uploaded or sent anywhere.
  • A file that comes from another app or a cloud drive is copied into the app’s storage on your device to be read. Such a copy is kept only while the file is in your recent files, so that you can open it again.
  • To read a compressed file (.gz), the app writes a temporary uncompressed copy to your device’s temporary storage and deletes it as soon as the file has been read.
  • Layer property files (.lyp) and marker files (.lyrdb) are saved only where you choose.
  • The demo chip that comes with LayoutCraft is written to the app’s own folder on your device.

Pictures you capture or share

Capture saves a picture of the view on your device, in Pictures/LayoutCraft. Share hands a picture of the view to the app you choose in Android’s share sheet, and only when you tap it.

Data kept on your device

The app keeps its settings on your device, in its own storage:

  • display and panel preferences, such as panel sizes, interface scale, theme, screen brightness and ruler snapping;
  • your recent files (the ones you pinned and up to 10 others) with a small picture of each for the start screen, and the folders you added or last used, so you can reopen them quickly.

This information stays on your device and is never sent to me or anyone else. On Android it is deleted when you clear the app’s storage or uninstall the app; a folder you added can also be removed in the app’s file browser. On a computer it is kept in your user profile’s application data folder (on Windows: %APPDATA%\rawrranger\LayoutCraft) until you delete it.

Permissions

LayoutCraft asks for no permissions. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or other personal information, and it does not need internet access. It reads the battery level only to show it beside the optional clock, on your device.

Information Google Play may share with me

If you have chosen in your Android settings to share usage and diagnostics data, Google may give me anonymous, aggregated statistics about crashes, app performance and installs (Android vitals). This data is collected by Google, not by LayoutCraft, under Google’s Privacy Policy. It does not tell me who you are, and I use it only to fix problems in the app.

If you contact me

If you email me, I receive your email address and what you write. I use them only to answer you, do not share them, and delete the conversation when it is no longer needed.

Children

LayoutCraft is an engineering tool and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Your choices

I hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for me to show, export or delete. You control everything the app keeps on your device and can remove it at any time by clearing the app’s storage, uninstalling the app, or deleting its settings folder.

Changes to this policy

If a version of LayoutCraft ever handles data differently, I will update this page and its “Last updated” date before that version is released.
